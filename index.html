01 / how it works कैसे चलता है

Three hops. Nothing else in the way.

SCROLL
  1. STEP 1 / 3

    Tap

    Your Android phone sends one small request with the preset id. The preset holds side, offset and stake.

  2. STEP 2 / 3

    Check

    The server reads the live price, moves it by your ticks, then checks stake, session token and the duplicate lock.

  3. STEP 3 / 3

    Place

    The bet goes out through the official API. The answer, accepted or rejected, lands in your bet log.

    03 / built not to miss, not to double सुरक्षा

    Boring on purpose. That is the point.

    Real money needs plain, careful code. These checks run on every bet. Nobody can promise perfect uptime or profit, so the service reports honestly what happened.

    Session renewal

    The login token is renewed before it runs out, and the retry is logged.

    taptap

    Duplicate lock

    A second tap or repeated poll inside the lock window is ignored and logged.

    wanted 1.98now 2.10

    Final price check

    Price and stake are checked a last time. Moved too far, nothing is sent.

    Clear error log

    Every accepted, rejected or blocked request is written down with a reason.
